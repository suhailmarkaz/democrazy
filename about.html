WHY DEMOCRAZYMADE FOR EVERY KIND OF CREATIVE

There is no
one way to
be brilliant.

Some people find their voice on a stage. Others find it in a sketchbook, a camera, a lyric, a line of code or a well-made argument.

DEMOCRAZY is built around that simple idea: talent has many forms, and every form deserves a space to be seen. It is a meeting point for students who make, perform, question, collaborate and try something they have never tried before.

Come to compete, come to support your friends, or come to discover a side of campus life you have not met yet. The best parts of a fest happen between the events, too — in the conversations, the backstage moments and the people you meet along the way.

THE DEMOCRAZY SPIRITTHREE THINGS WE BELIEVE
01

Make it
your own.

Bring your point of view. Original ideas, unexpected approaches and brave first attempts belong here.

02

Make room
for others.

A great fest is not only about the people on stage. It is also about the people cheering, helping and sharing the space.

03

Make it
count.

Give every performance, entry and encounter your best. The experience matters as much as the result.
